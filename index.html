About

Who I am

An engineer who likes questions with a measurable answer.

I am an AI engineer based in Bengaluru. I started as an engineering intern in January 2025, moved into a full-time role that summer, and now help customers across EMEA build their AI solutions, from discovery to production.

Most of my day is the gap between a prototype and something a customer can rely on: scoping the problem in the first conversation, building the agents, retrieval and serving layers, and deploying them on OCI. I have built systems for government and tax-authority clients, including multilingual assistants and image-matching APIs that run on a CPU-only machine.

Outside client work I run small, controlled studies on the same questions: how many passages an agent should retrieve, which serving flag actually helps, when a cheap model should hand off to a strong one. Each is published here with its numbers and its limits.

I studied electronics at JSS Science and Technology University, where I published papers on seismic signal analysis, IoT intrusion detection and satellite link design, and hold a design patent. That is where the habit comes from: state the question, fix the variables, report the interval.
